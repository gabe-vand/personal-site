Savor

A pantry that knows what you have, and what is about to go bad.

About 38% of the US food supply goes uneaten — roughly $473 billion a year, and the single largest category in landfill. Savor photographs your receipt, reads it with Gemini’s vision model and turns it into an inventory; then it suggests meals built around whatever is closest to expiring, and keeps a running tally of the CO₂ and the money you didn’t throw away. Built over one weekend with the three people in that photograph, none of whom had written a React component before Friday.

Reading a dog’s gait

Point it at a video of a dog walking. It tells you whether the knee is torn.

A cranial cruciate ligament tear is the most common orthopaedic injury in dogs and it is normally diagnosed by eye. So this is two pieces. First I built the keypointing: a two-stage cascade that tracks 39 body keypoints and 46 facial landmarks on a dog in motion, frame by frame, and it lands mean landmark error 28.6% below the single-model version it replaced. Then I trained the thing that actually answers the question — those joint trajectories become gait features, and a model trained on 12 GB of clinical video calls CCL against normal. It refuses footage that isn’t shot side-on, and hands back a pose-overlaid copy of the video so a clinician can see what it was looking at rather than trust a number.

032026Raspberry Pi

Oracle

A Minecraft server on a Raspberry Pi, and something living on it that has been paying attention.

The server is a Paper instance on a Raspberry Pi 4B in my house, reachable by friends over the public internet through an outbound tunnel rather than a hole in my router, and it restarts itself without ever losing the world. Oracle is the Java plugin on top. It has no command and no prefix: it reads every public chat line, scores it 0–100 on whether it is worth answering, and speaks only when a line clears the bar and it is off cooldown — the logs really do read INTERJECT (interest 88) and SILENT (interest 12 below bar of 15). It notices deaths, advancements and slower patterns, and the model behind it swaps between Claude, Gemini and the Jetson downstairs without a restart. It is quiet most of the time. That is the design.

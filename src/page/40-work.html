Savor

A pantry that knows what you have, and what is about to go bad.

About 38% of the US food supply goes uneaten — the largest single category in landfill. Savor photographs your receipt, reads it with Gemini’s vision model, and turns it into a pantry that suggests meals around whatever is closest to expiring. Built in one weekend with the three people in that photograph, none of whom had written a React component before Friday.

Reading a dog’s gait

Point it at a video of a dog walking. It tells you whether the knee is torn.

A torn cranial cruciate ligament is the most common orthopaedic injury in dogs, and it is normally called by eye. I built the keypointing first — a two-stage cascade that tracks the dog frame by frame, 28.6% more accurately than the model it replaced — then trained the classifier that reads gait from it on 12 GB of clinical video. It hands back a pose-overlaid copy of your video, so a vet can check its work.

Oracle

A Minecraft server on a Raspberry Pi, and something living on it that listens.

A Paper server running on a Raspberry Pi 4B in my house, open to friends over an outbound tunnel rather than a hole in my router. Oracle is the Java plugin on top: it reads every line of chat, scores it 0–100 on whether it is worth answering, and speaks only when one clears the bar — with Claude, Gemini or the Jetson downstairs behind it. It is quiet most of the time. That is the design.
